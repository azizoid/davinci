# Inbox

Place raw source recordings here, or drop them into the local control panel. The intended interaction is then simply:

press **Edit Video**

The future workflow will discover the recording and perform all intermediate editing operations autonomously. Saying `edit this` to Codex is an equivalent trigger. Optional instructions may be supplied as a clearly associated sidecar file, but are not required for a standard edit.

Do not place pipeline-generated intermediates or final renders here.
